The brief

The content in plain English — you do the writing
Economy
Vietnam (emerging). This paragraph: opening to trade and foreign investment, begun under the Đổi Mới reforms of 1986 and deepened by joining the WTO in 2007.
Argument
Opening up has driven fast growth and lifted millions of people out of poverty.
Counterpoint
The gains have been uneven (cities and the coast more than rural areas), growth has harmed the environment, and the economy is exposed when world demand falls.
Chain stepsUse in this part
Trade barriers cut and foreign firms welcomed → foreign firms build factories → more capital and new technology → workers produce more and manufacturing jobs grow → exports rise → GDP grows → incomes rise → less poverty, better health and education.

Limitation

1 · Topic Sentence

While trade liberalisation and FDI attraction since Đổi Mới have been highly effective in accelerating Vietnam’s economic growth, their contribution to development has been qualified by rising inequality and environmental degradation.

2 · Theoretical Mechanism

Under the Đổi Mới reforms, Vietnam cut trade barriers and welcomed foreign direct investment, prompting foreign firms to build factories. This inflow raised the capital stock and transferred new technology, increasing labour productivity and expanding manufacturing employment. Higher productivity made Vietnamese goods more competitive, so exports rose, lifting net exports (X − M) and shifting aggregate demand to the right, which increased real GDP. As incomes rose, households moved out of poverty and gained better access to health and education.
