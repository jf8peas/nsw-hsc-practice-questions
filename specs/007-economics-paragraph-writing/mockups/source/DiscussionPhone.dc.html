The model paragraph

This part

While trade liberalisation and FDI attraction since Đổi Mới have been highly effective in accelerating Vietnam’s economic growth, their contribution to development has been qualified by rising inequality and environmental degradation. Under the Đổi Mới reforms, Vietnam cut trade barriers and welcomed foreign direct investment, prompting foreign firms to build factories. This inflow raised the capital stock and transferred new technology, increasing labour productivity and expanding manufacturing employment. Higher productivity made Vietnamese goods more competitive, so exports rose, lifting net exports (X − M) and shifting aggregate demand to the right, which increased real GDP. As incomes rose, households moved out of poverty and gained better access to health and education. The data confirm this chain.
