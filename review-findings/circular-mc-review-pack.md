## m145 — Barker 2020 Trial, Q6
**Question:** Which of the following statements is INCORRECT about the work being done on an object undergoing circular motion in a horizontal plane?

**Options:**
- A: No work is being done as the total energy is constant due to the potential energy and kinetic energy being constant.
- B: Force and displacement are perpendicular at each instant, so there is no displacement in the direction of the force and so W = Fs = 0.
- C: No work is being done as the total energy is constant due to the fact that the speed and height of the object do not change.
- D: Displacement is in the direction of the force and so W = Fs = a constant, causing work to be done on the object.

**KEYED ANSWER:** D

**WHY (key):** The centripetal force is always perpendicular to the displacement (velocity), so it does no work; the kinetic and potential energies stay constant. The statement claiming work is done is the incorrect one.

**Question:** A tether ball is swinging around a pole in uniform circular motion on a horizontal plane as shown below.
Which of the following expression would correctly determine the mass of the ball?

**Options:**
- A: m = F_T cos θ / g
- B: m = √(F_c r / v)

**Diagram:** A ball on a string tied to the top of a vertical pole moves in a horizontal circle around the pole. The string makes angle θ with the pole. Two forces act on the ball: the tension F_T along the string towards the pole top, and the weight F_g straight down.

## m180 — Sydney Girls 2020 Trial, Q5
**Question:** Car A of mass m can take a curve of radius r at a maximum speed v before skidding. Car B of mass 1.5m can take the same curve at a maximum speed of:

**Options:**
- A: 0.8v
- B: v
- C: 1.2v
- D: 1.5v

**KEYED ANSWER:** C

**WHY (key):** The same maximum force F = mv²/r on the same curve gives m v² constant: v_B = v√(m_A/m_B) = v√1.5 ≈ 1.2v.
