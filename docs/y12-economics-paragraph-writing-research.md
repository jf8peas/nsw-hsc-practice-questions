# Y12 Economics paragraph writing: sources (Principle V)

Unit: `y12-economics-paragraph-writing` (spec 007). Syllabus: NSW HSC Economics (2009 syllabus,
examined in 2027), Topic 1 The Global Economy; aligned to the Year 12 Economics Assessment Task 1
notification (globalisation's influence on an emerging and a developing economy; strategies since
1980 to promote growth and development). Essays are original; no real HSC question is used; the
essay questions are general ("an emerging economy you have studied"). Sources below are for the
briefs' facts only and are **not** shown to students.

Every figure in a brief must be re-checked against its source before an essay ships, and the date
recorded as `figuresVerified` in `content/units/y12-economics-paragraph-writing/essays.ts`.

### e05 Nepal: labour migration and remittances (dependence)

- About US$11 billion in remittances in 2023, over a quarter of GDP (World Bank data: about US$10.8 billion, 26.2% of GDP). Corrected 23 Nov 2026 from US$9.6 billion, which matched no World Bank figure: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remittances_to_Nepal ; https://www.theglobaleconomy.com/Nepal/remittances_percent_GDP/
- 76.8% of households received remittances, NLSS IV 2022/23: https://nepjol.info/index.php/DSAJ/article/view/84892/67372
- Poverty 25.16% (2010/11) to 20.27% (2022/23), NLSS IV: https://asianews.network/?p=134515 . Note: the World Bank says the 2023 and 2011 aggregates are not strictly comparable; the brief reports the survey office's own comparison.
- Without remittances, an estimated 2.6 million more people in poverty (World Bank Macro Poverty Outlook): https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/5d1783db09a0e09d15bbcea8ef0cec0b-0500052021/related/mpo-npl.pdf
- 72.4% of household remittances used for daily consumption, 15.8% for loan repayment, NLSS IV 2022/23. Corrected 23 Nov 2026 from "around 90%", which overstated the survey: https://ekantipur.com/news/2024/06/18/en/768-percent-of-households-receiving-remittances-lumbini-is-the-first-37-22.html
- Youth (15-24) unemployment 22.7% in 2022/23, NLSS IV (NSO): https://kathmandupost.com/money/2024/06/16/nepal-s-unemployment-rate-rises-to-12-6-percent-survey-says ; drives emigration (World Bank Poverty and Equity Brief): https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051101062533877/pdf/IDU-7b8e9555-a244-4fe7-9608-28aa787130cc.pdf
