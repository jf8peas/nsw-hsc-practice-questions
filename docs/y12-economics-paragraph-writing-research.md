# Y12 Economics paragraph writing: sources (Principle V)

Unit: `y12-economics-paragraph-writing` (spec 007). Syllabus: NSW HSC Economics (2009 syllabus,
examined in 2027), Topic 1 The Global Economy; aligned to the Year 12 Economics Assessment Task 1
notification (globalisation's influence on an emerging and a developing economy; strategies since
1980 to promote growth and development). Essays are original; no real HSC question is used; the
essay questions are general ("an emerging economy you have studied"). Sources below are for the
briefs' facts only and are **not** shown to students.

Every figure in a brief must be re-checked against its source before an essay ships, and the date
recorded as `figuresVerified` in `content/units/y12-economics-paragraph-writing/essays.ts`.
