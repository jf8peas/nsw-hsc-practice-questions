# Y11 Economics Preliminary — milestone test research

Unit: `y11-economics-preliminary` — broad revision for the **Economics Y11
Preliminary** exam. 5 multiple-choice + 5 short-answer per attempt, drawn from a
bank of 50 + 50 (10 non-repeating attempts). MC is 1 mark; short answers are
2–4 marks and LLM-graded.

## Sources

- **Syllabus:** `resources/topic002/NESA - economics_11_12_2025 (S6).pdf` — the
  current NESA Economics 11–12 Syllabus. Year 11 is six focus areas
  (indicative hours in brackets):
  1. Introduction to economics (25)
  2. Markets (35)
  3. Household and business sector (15)
  4. Financial sector (15)
  5. Government sector (15)
  6. International sector (15)

  scope only.**

## Old-exam relevance

The past papers pre-date the 2025 syllabus (they follow the 2009 Stage 6
syllabus). The Year 11 micro core — scarcity, opportunity cost, PPF, circular
flow (`S + T + M = I + G + X`), demand & supply, elasticity, market failure,
government roles, taxation, financial markets, trade — carries over almost
unchanged, so those exam questions are still on-syllabus.

What has shifted, and is handled by writing to the **2025** syllabus rather
than copying old questions:

- The 2025 course adds **behavioural economics / cognitive biases**, an explicit
  **production possibilities model**, fuller **market-structure** coverage
  (perfect competition → monopoly), and a broader **market-failure** list
  (asymmetric information, market-power abuse, inequality).
- Some macro-flavoured content in the old prelim papers (detailed
  unemployment types, monetary/fiscal policy analysis, exchange-rate
  determination in depth) is now **Year 12**; those questions were not used.
- Institutions named in old papers (specific regulators, policy settings) are
  avoided in favour of syllabus concepts.

Questions in the bank are original, written to the 2025 syllabus dot points,
in the command-word style of a NSW preliminary exam ("Define…", "Explain…",
"Distinguish…", "Outline…", "Calculate…").
